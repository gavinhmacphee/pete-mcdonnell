About

Built on the training ground.

Peter McDonnell is an Academy Head Coach at Philadelphia Union with a coaching path that runs from the Football Association of Ireland to NCAA Division I to MLS Next. He has worked at every level of youth soccer in America: grassroots, club, high school, NCAA, and MLS academy.

He holds the UEFA A License from the Football Association of Ireland, the US Soccer Academy Director License, and the Elite Formation Coaching License, a 2024 program for top-level MLS coaches run jointly with the French Football Federation. The work is built around one belief: development is a system, not a moment, and players reveal potential when the environment demands it.

What I Do

Three ways to work together.

Each engagement starts with a conversation. From there we shape something that fits the player, the staff, or the club. Never a template.

For Players

Elite Player Mentorship

An individual development plan built around video, decision-making, and what the next level actually demands. We watch your game, name the gaps that matter, and design the work that closes them.

Best for MLS Next, ECNL, and academy players targeting the professional or collegiate pathway.

For Coaches

Coaching Methodology Mentorship

The way you coach is a system: how you plan a session, how you give feedback, how you set the standard players meet every day. We work through your methodology and the environment you create, from session principles to the small interactions that decide whether players develop.

Best for Club, academy, and high school coaches who want to sharpen how they develop players.
Discuss this →
For Clubs

Club Development Audit

A structured review of how your club identifies, progresses, and graduates players. We look at the pathway end-to-end and surface where alignment breaks (in selection, programming, or coach-to-coach handoff) and what to do about it.

Best for Directors of Coaching and academy leadership building (or rebuilding) a player pathway.
