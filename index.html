What I Do

Three ways to work together.

Each one starts with a conversation. From there we figure out what fits the player, the coach, or the club. Nothing off the shelf.

For Players

Working with a Player

Individual work with a player who's serious about the next level. We watch your game on film, talk through what we see, and put together what you should be working on between sessions.

Best for Players in MLS Next, ECNL, or academy programs working toward college or pro.
Get in touch →
For Coaches

Working with a Coach

One-to-one work with a coach who wants to get sharper. We watch your sessions, look at how you're planning and how you're coaching in the moment, and talk through what we see. You walk away with a clearer sense of what to keep and what to change.

Best for Club, academy, or high school coaches who want to get better at the daily craft.
Get in touch →
For Clubs

Working with a Club

A look at how your club identifies players and moves them through the pathway. We go through what's working, where good players are getting stuck, and what's happening at the handoffs between age groups. You get a written report at the end with what we found and our recommendations.

Best for DOCs and academy leadership who are building or rebuilding a player pathway.

About

A working coach.

Peter coaches at the Philadelphia Union Academy. He started as a grassroots coach in Ireland, then came to the US for a master's degree and a college coaching role at the University of Maine. He's coached at every level of the American game since, from grassroots through to MLS academy.

Peter believes player development happens at training, not in matches. The way a session runs, the feedback a player gets, the day-to-day standard. That's the level where the work matters most.
